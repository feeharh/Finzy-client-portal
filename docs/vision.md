# Vision

# Product Name

**Working Name:** Finzy Client Portal

> A modern client management platform for alteration businesses, designed to simplify appointments, measurements, garment tracking, communication, and customer experience.

---

# Vision Statement

Our vision is to modernize the alteration industry by replacing paper records, scattered WhatsApp conversations, spreadsheets, and manual appointment management with a centralized digital platform.

The platform empowers both customers and alteration businesses by providing a seamless experience from appointment booking to garment pickup while preserving customer measurements, garment history, and communication in one place.

Long-term, the platform aims to become the operating system for alteration businesses worldwide.

---

# The Problem

Today, most alteration businesses manage operations through a combination of:

- WhatsApp conversations
- Phone calls
- Paper measurement books
- Notes apps
- Photo galleries
- Manual appointment scheduling
- Memory

This results in:

- Lost measurements
- Missed appointments
- Repeated customer questions
- Poor garment tracking
- Difficult communication
- No centralized customer history
- Time-consuming administrative work

Customers also experience frustration because they:

- Don't know the status of their garments
- Cannot easily retrieve their measurements
- Have no digital history of previous alterations
- Must repeatedly provide the same information

---

# Our Solution

The platform provides one centralized system where alteration businesses can manage their entire workflow while giving customers visibility into every stage of the process.

The platform includes:

- Appointment booking
- Customer profiles
- Measurement vault
- Garment management
- Appointment tracking
- Status updates
- Quotes
- Communication history
- Notifications
- Calendar integration
- WhatsApp communication
- Secure customer portal

---

# Core Features

## Customer Portal

Customers can:

- Request appointments
- Upload garment photos and videos
- View appointment status
- Access measurements
- Download measurement profiles
- Add appointments to their calendars
- Receive notifications
- View alteration history
- Access their profile through a secure private link

---

## Administrator Portal

Administrators can:

- Manage appointments
- Manage customers
- Store measurement profiles
- Track garments
- Upload garment photos
- Send notifications
- Communicate through WhatsApp
- Generate measurement reports
- Maintain customer history

---

## Measurement Vault

Customer measurements become digital assets that can be securely accessed at any time.

Measurement profiles are versioned, allowing businesses and customers to maintain historical records as measurements change over time.

---

## Garment Tracking

Customers can track garments throughout the alteration process.

Example workflow:

- Request Submitted
- Approved
- Garment Received
- In Progress
- Ready for Fitting
- Ready for Pickup
- Completed

---

## Communication Center

Every appointment includes a communication timeline.

Administrators can:

- Open WhatsApp conversations
- Send emails
- Send SMS
- Record internal notes

All communication is linked to the customer's appointment history.

---

## Calendar Integration

Customers can add approved appointments directly to:

- Google Calendar
- Apple Calendar
- Outlook

This reduces missed appointments and improves customer experience.

# Marketplace Vision

Future versions of the platform will support marketplace purchases.

Customers who purchase clothing from platforms such as:

- Depop
- Vinted
- Poshmark
- eBay
- Facebook Marketplace
- Other resale marketplaces

will be able to request alteration services directly through the platform.

Customers will be able to:

- Import or upload listing information
- Use saved measurements
- Request alteration estimates
- Track alterations after purchase

Future AI capabilities may recommend whether a garment can be altered before the customer purchases it.

---

# Future Vision

- Fashion designers

Each business will manage its own customers while using the same platform.
