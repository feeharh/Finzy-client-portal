# Finzy Client Portal

## Vision

Finzy Client Portal is a web-based client management platform designed for alteration businesses.

The platform centralizes appointment booking, measurement management, garment tracking, customer communication, and alteration history into a single system.

The goal is to eliminate manual processes currently managed through WhatsApp, text messages, spreadsheets, paper measurement books, and memory.

---

## Problem Statement

Most alteration businesses manage customers using:

- WhatsApp messages
- Phone calls
- Paper measurement books
- Manual appointment scheduling
- Photo galleries on mobile devices

This creates operational challenges:

- Lost measurements
- Missed appointments
- Repeated customer questions
- Poor communication tracking
- Lack of garment status visibility
- No customer history

---

## Solution

Provide a centralized platform where:

### Customers can

- Request appointments
- Upload garment photos
- View appointment status
- Access measurement records
- Receive notifications
- Track garment progress

### Administrators can

- Manage appointments
- Store measurements
- Track garments
- Manage customer communication
- Generate measurement records
- Maintain customer history

---

## Success Metrics

### Business Metrics

- Reduce customer status inquiry messages by 50%
- Reduce appointment scheduling time by 80%
- Eliminate paper measurement records

### Technical Metrics

- 99% uptime
- Secure customer data access
